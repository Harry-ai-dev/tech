Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to your key questions

Here are the answers to the most common questions.

1

Why are we looking for a partner?

Our team has grown steadily and worked with clients worldwide.

We need a trustworthy partner in Europe to bridge the gap. Salaries in our country are lower than in Europe or America. We aim for fair partnerships where both sides grow together.

2

Why are we going to use your account?

Salaries in Asian countries are generally lower than in Europe or America.

We use your account to earn more while working in the same environment.

We will not use your account for any bad or illegal purpose, and you can check what we are doing in real time.

3

Why are we going to use your computer?

Many freelance websites detect users based on IP addresses.

We need to use your IP address through your computer.

You may worry about your data. Don't worry. You can make sure we cannot access your personal data with a simple option.

4

How do we make sure your personal data is safe?

We will work on your computer remotely using a virtual machine called VMware.

It provides another computer environment, completely separated from your personal data. The virtual machine uses a different operating system, keeping your data safe.

5

How can you trust us?

All payments are processed through your bank account, and you can control your payment account yourself.

When the project is finished, you will receive payment from the client and then share the agreed amount with us. All transactions are under your supervision and control.

6

You don't have to know about technology.

Your role is only to create an account, verify it, allow us to work on your computer, and share the agreed payment with us.

There are no technical requirements for you during this process.

7

What is your role? What do you have to do?

  1. Create your account.
  2. Verify your account and make it secure.
  3. Install VMware and allow us to work on your computer. Keep it powered on when needed.
  4. Notify us when payment arrives and share agreed amount.
8

Which kind of payment system do we use?

We can use different payment systems depending on the project.

You can use PayPal or another mutually agreed payment method.

9

What kinds of work does your team do?

We provide software development services for various industries: Web development, Android apps, AI-powered apps, backend systems, APIs, database development, DevOps, UI implementation, testing, maintenance, bug fixing, and support.

We handle projects from planning to deployment and maintenance. Experience with both small and large, long-term projects.

10

What if your computer performance is not good?

You may need to install VMware and allocate hardware resources, so performance may be lower at first.

After one or two months, we will buy a new computer for you, and you can allow us to work on that computer. This will be safer for your data and more comfortable for you.
